Field

Choose a field to run your creatures through.

⚖️

Council

Pose a dilemma; creatures take positions, debate, and (with a mediator) seek a resolution.

🗣️

Open Council

Pose a dilemma; creatures speak in turn — no mediator, no forced resolution. Free deliberation.

📋

Forum

State a claim; creatures rate their confidence, bring evidence, challenge, and revise.

🎓

Academy

Set a theme; creatures study, discuss, and teach one another — learning together, not debating to win.

🌲

Wilderness

A living world of events — storms, discoveries, strangers. Creatures act tick by tick; you watch what they learn.

💬

Agora

Two creatures meet in the open square and talk freely — no task, no winner. A conversation that leaves a bond.
