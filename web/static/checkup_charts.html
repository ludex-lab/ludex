creature.chart — indicator panel

Off-substrate aggregation of indicators/ · ← ledger grid · verdict chains · local only (private creature data)
Series are segmented by brain epoch — a re-brain breaks the line rather than joining across it (epoch grammar), and the segment count is shown when a creature has more than one. Activity-dependent indicators always print their coverage: an empty indicator means "no activity in the window", never "healthy". Open prescriptions appear at the bottom of a card; one crossed by an epoch split is flagged for review.
